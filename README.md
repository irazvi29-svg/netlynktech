# netlynktech

## Intake form email delivery

The intake form sends submissions directly to `imran@netlynktech.com` through FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint. Visitors do not need to open an email app or copy a dispatch packet.

Before going live, submit a test inquiry and approve the activation email FormSubmit sends to the recipient address. FormSubmit cannot deliver inquiries until the mailbox owner verifies the address. Form data is processed by FormSubmit; review its privacy terms before using this integration.
